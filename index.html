Questions before you order

How long does printing take?

Most orders print within a few business days of your payment being confirmed, depending on order size and current queue. You'll get a heads-up if a piece needs longer.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. Interac e-Transfer runs through your own bank's app, not through us, and deposits directly into a verified account. We never see or store your banking details.

What if I'm not happy with my print?

Send us a message with a photo and we'll sort it out — a reprint or a refund, whichever makes sense for the issue.

What colours can I choose?

Every product on this site is available in Black, White, Red, Blue, or Brown (Wood). Pick your colour right on the product card before adding to cart.
