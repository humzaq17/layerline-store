How ordering works

STEP 1

Pick colour & quantity

Choose a filament colour and how many you want, then hit Buy Now to open your order summary.

STEP 2

Send an Interac e-Transfer

Your order screen shows the exact amount and the e-Transfer email to send it to, plus your order number as the reference.

STEP 3

We print & ship

Once the e-Transfer is received, your item goes into the print queue and ships out with tracking.
